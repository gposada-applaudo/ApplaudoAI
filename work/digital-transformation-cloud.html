Client Government Agencies
Industry Public Sector

The project.

Multiple government agencies operating in isolation, each with different digital maturity levels — and a national mandate to unify them under a single cloud strategy.

Capabilities
Google Cloud Strategic Consulting Systems Integration Infrastructure Modernization Cloud Migration Data Analytics & Visualization

National digital transformation doesn't happen in a single agency — it happens across all of them, simultaneously, with each one moving at a different pace. This engagement was about creating a shared foundation: a cloud architecture, a data strategy, and a governance model that every agency could build on without losing its operational autonomy.

We partnered with government experts to develop a roadmap that was realistic about where agencies were starting from, not just where they needed to go. The result was a modular, scalable system that reduced redundancy and created the conditions for true cross-agency collaboration.

The challenge.

Four systemic problems, each blocking the others.

Heterogeneity of needs.

Agencies operated under vastly different models and maturity levels. Aligning them under a single digital strategy while preserving each agency's distinct operational requirements made standardization nearly impossible from the start.

Undefined end-state.

Agencies struggled to articulate what their future processes should look like. Without a clear target, technical requirements remained misaligned and implementation decisions were made without a shared objective to anchor them.

Siloed execution.

Digital projects ran in isolation across agencies, slowing down learning and preventing the capture of synergies. Fragmented delivery created barriers that stopped teams from sharing insights or reusing solutions across the national effort.

No standardization layer.

The absence of shared architecture, governance, and security protocols forced each agency to solve the same problems independently — wasting resources and blocking the economies of scale that a coordinated approach could have unlocked.

One strategy. Every agency.

We aligned government experts and agency stakeholders around a shared digital roadmap before touching any infrastructure. Applaudo used data-driven frameworks to help each agency visualize its own transformation goals, then connected those goals to a unified cloud architecture that could serve all of them.

Strategy alignment & product definition

Through iterative working sessions with each agency, we helped teams move from ambiguous objectives to concrete technical requirements. The process surfaced cross-agency dependencies early, turning what had been individual roadblocks into shared design inputs for the platform.

Cloud infrastructure & cross-agency integration

We implemented cloud infrastructure and data-sharing mechanisms that centralized information while preserving each agency's operational independence. Agencies could access shared services and contribute to shared datasets without being forced into identical workflows.

Governance, security & standardization

A unified governance layer — incorporating cloud architecture standards, data protocols, and cybersecurity controls — gave agencies a framework they could adopt at their own pace. Standardization became a benefit rather than a constraint, enabling cost-effective decisions across the entire national portfolio.

A shared cloud foundation designed for agencies at different stages of digital maturity.

What we shipped.

One national strategy. Measurable impact across every agency.

Unified national digital strategy across all participating agencies
Seamless interoperability enabling cross-agency data exchange
