The project.

A high-growth energy company with rising operational costs, fragmented customer data, and sales processes that couldn't keep pace with the business's ambitions.

Capabilities
Salesforce Consulting Scalable Cloud Architecture Partner Experience Optimization Data-Driven Operations Agile Delivery & QA

Scaling a business is hard when the tools meant to support growth become friction instead. Manual processes, disconnected systems, and inconsistent data were slowing down response times, extending sales cycles, and making it difficult for leadership to see what was actually happening across the business.

We built a Salesforce-centered ecosystem that unified customer and partner data, automated core business functions, and created a data-enabled environment where leaders could act on real-time insights rather than last week's reports. The result was a platform the business could grow on — not just for today's volume, but for the next stage.

The challenge.

Four inefficiencies compounding each other.

Slow customer response.

Fragmented systems forced teams to manually pull and reconcile data before responding to customer inquiries. Every step added latency, increased error rates, and eroded the quality of the customer experience at the moments that mattered most.

Product delivery too slow.

Getting new features to market required manual coordination across teams, extended QA cycles, and deployment processes that hadn't been built for speed. The business was ready to move faster — its delivery infrastructure wasn't.

Rising operational costs.

Inefficiencies accumulated across every business function — from sales support to reporting. Without visibility into where time and resources were being absorbed, there was no clear path to reduction without cutting capabilities alongside waste.

Disconnected partner and employee tools.

Internal teams and sales partners were operating in different systems with no shared source of truth. Productivity suffered and engagement declined as people spent more time managing tools than doing the work those tools were supposed to support.

One platform. Every workflow.

We designed and implemented a Salesforce-driven ecosystem that automated core business functions, unified customer and partner data, and integrated seamlessly with the company's existing operations. Agile delivery frameworks and CI/CD pipelines ran in parallel — accelerating product rollout without trading away quality.

Salesforce automation & data unification

We consolidated fragmented customer and partner data into a single Salesforce environment, then built automated workflows to handle the manual coordination steps that had been slowing down response times. Customer inquiries, partner dashboards, and sales pipeline management all ran from a single source of truth.

Agile delivery & CI/CD pipelines

To close the gap between feature development and production deployment, we introduced automated testing and CI/CD pipelines tuned for the company's release cadence. Teams could ship new capabilities without the extended coordination overhead that had been adding weeks to every product cycle.

Cloud infrastructure & operational visibility

The underlying cloud architecture was rebuilt for scalability and cost efficiency, ensuring the platform could support business growth without proportional infrastructure spend. Leadership gained real-time dashboards that surfaced the operational metrics they needed to make faster, better-informed decisions.

A unified Salesforce ecosystem giving leadership real-time visibility across customers, partners, and operations.

What changed.

Faster service. Faster delivery. Lower cost to operate.

Faster Customer Service & Revenue Growth.

Automated processes eliminated manual data errors, enabling timely and accurate responses to customer inquiries — strengthening relationships and shortening the path from inquiry to close.

Accelerated Time-to-Market.

Agile delivery practices and automated pipelines reduced lead times for new features, allowing the business to respond to market changes without the delays that had previously extended every release cycle.

Lower Operational Costs.

Streamlined workflows and shared infrastructure freed resources previously absorbed by inefficiency — redirecting budget toward innovation and long-term growth rather than maintaining the status quo.
