Enhancing Safety Protocols with Artificial Intelligence.

Client Construction Company
Industry Manufacturing
Services Data · Artificial Intelligence
Year 2024

The project.

A construction company with active sites, high foot traffic, and an existing monitoring system — but no reliable way to detect non-compliance with safety gear requirements in real time.

Capabilities
AI Solution Identification Opportunity Assessment Cloud Migration Strategy Real-Time Analytics

Hazardous environments don't give second chances. When a worker isn't wearing their helmet, the window between detection and intervention is measured in seconds — not reports or end-of-day reviews. This engagement was about building a system that could act in that window, reliably, across changing conditions.

We partnered with the client's operations and safety teams to deploy an AI-powered detection solution that integrated directly into their existing monitoring infrastructure. The goal: zero disruption to site workflows while delivering immediate, actionable safety alerts.

The challenge.

Four problems that made passive monitoring insufficient.

Enforcing compliance in real time.

Manual monitoring couldn't reliably detect helmet non-compliance across active sites with changing conditions and multiple camera feeds. Enforcement was reactive, not proactive — incidents were discovered after the fact.

Preventing accidents before they happen.

Rapid identification of non-compliant workers was critical to reducing accidents, worker injuries, and liability. The system needed to flag violations while there was still time to intervene — not after an incident was already in motion.

Accuracy in dynamic conditions.

High foot traffic, variable lighting, and changing site configurations meant detection needed to be adaptive, not just trained. The system had to maintain high accuracy even as the environment around it shifted throughout the workday.

Integration without disruption.

A new AI layer could only succeed if it fit seamlessly into existing monitoring workflows. Any solution that required replacing infrastructure or retraining teams from scratch would face resistance and delay impact from day one.

Real-time detection, built to last.

We deployed a YOLO-based (You Only Look Once) object detection system integrated with cloud-native architecture. The solution streamed live video feeds, identified non-compliant workers without helmets in real time, and triggered immediate alerts to site supervisors — all within the existing monitoring workflow.

Computer vision & model deployment

The YOLO model was fine-tuned on site-specific conditions, accounting for the range of lighting, distances, and worker densities present on the client's construction sites. We structured the inference pipeline to process streams with minimal latency, ensuring alert delivery in time for a supervisor to act.

Continuous learning & feedback loops

Post-deployment, we built automated retraining pipelines that incorporated flagged edge cases back into the model. Each feedback loop improved detection accuracy across the scenarios the original training data hadn't fully captured — especially partially obstructed views and low-light conditions.

Cloud infrastructure & scalability

The entire detection and alerting stack was built on cloud-native infrastructure, enabling deployment across multiple sites without per-site hardware investment. Updates to the model and alerting logic could be rolled out centrally, ensuring consistency across the operation.

YOLO-based detection running on live video streams — flagging non-compliance the moment it occurs.

What changed.

Proactive safety, not reactive reporting.

Improved Safety Compliance.

Accurate, real-time helmet detection with immediate alerts enabled prompt intervention — reinforcing protective equipment adherence across all monitored zones.

Reduced Accident Risk.

Proactive identification of non-compliant workers lowered the likelihood of injury, reducing downtime, liability exposure, and the human cost of preventable incidents.

Scalable, Self-Improving Monitoring.

Cloud infrastructure and automated retraining loops ensured sustained detection accuracy and easy rollout to new sites — without additional hardware or manual model updates.
